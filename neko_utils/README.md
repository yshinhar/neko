# 🐾 Neko V2: The Mind-Blasting Desktop Assistant

Welcome to the new and improved Neko! We've kept the core idea but turned it into a modern, powerful, and interactive companion.

## ✨ Mind-Blasting Features

1. **🐱 Floating Screen Pet**: Neko now lives on your screen as a sleek, transparent floating icon. You can drag him anywhere or double-click for quick actions.
2. **🖱️ Follow Mouse Mode**: Right-click and toggle "Follow Mouse" to make Neko chase your cursor around the screen!
3. **🎤 Voice Commands**: Neko is always listening!
   - Say **"Neko, work time"** to trigger your work automation.
   - Say **"Neko, calculate 5 plus 5"** for hands-free math.
   - Say **"Neko, follow"** or **"Neko, stop"** to control his movement.
4. **🧮 Smart Calculator**: Neko isn't just for math. He handles unit conversions too!
   - `200 cm to in`
   - `50 kg to lb`
   - All results are logged in your `calc_history.json`.
5. **🚀 Legacy Automation Support**: Your existing `automations.json` works perfectly. Neko will open your URLs and run your commands with a meow.
6. **🎵 Interactive Audio**: Neko purrs when he starts and meows when you click him or give commands.

## 🛠️ How to Run

Just run the UI script:
```bash
python neko_ui.py
```

## 🎮 Controls
- **Left Click**: Meow! (and triggers default automation if configured).
- **Right Click**: Open the magic menu for automations, settings, and calculator.
- **Double Click**: Quick-open the calculator.
- **Drag**: Move Neko anywhere on your desktop.
